🎬 Streaming Netflix or Prime?

If your screen share shows a black screen on Netflix, Amazon Prime, or Disney+, toggle off Hardware Acceleration in your browser:

Google Chrome / Edge / Brave Steps:
  1. Open Browser Settings.
  2. Search for "Hardware Acceleration".
  3. Turn OFF "Use graphics acceleration when available".
  4. Click Relaunch browser & share screen!
💡 Tip: Always select "Chrome Tab" when sharing and check "Share tab audio".
